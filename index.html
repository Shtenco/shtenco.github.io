07 / ENGINEERING CONSTITUTION

Принципы сильнее
слогановPrinciples over
slogans

Общая линия современных репозиториев — отделять гипотезу от доказательства, authority от presentation и внутреннее движение стоимости от внешней прибыли.The modern repository line separates hypothesis from evidence, authority from presentation, and internal value movement from external profit.

ONE AUTHORITY PER FACT

Accounting, settlement, inference, trust and presentation must not silently become competing sources of truth.

EVIDENCE BEFORE AUTHORITY

Evidence precedes recognition; recognition precedes accounting; capacity precedes authorization; authorization precedes execution.

AI PROPOSES

Deterministic policy constrains. Intelligence does not receive monetary authority merely because it can generate a confident answer.

INTERNAL ≠ EXTERNAL WEALTH

Mint, burn, own-token marks, internal transfers and flash principal are not external profit.

BLOCKCHAIN PROVES

Blockchain commits authoritative artifacts; it does not invent accounting or settlement truth.

CLAIMS REQUIRE EVIDENCE

Trading, network, compression, AI and scientific claims are strongest when exact artifacts and matched baselines can be reproduced.
